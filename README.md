### 🏆 Things I'm unreasonably proud of

- ⚡ A report screen was reading **298 work orders to show 16**. I had a word with it. Page load went from **1.79s to 0.68s (-62%)**, saves stopped waiting on a 7–60s recompute, and the output stayed identical. Rolled out to 4 production lines.
- 🔌 Taught **biometric attendance machines** to talk to a web app over the ADMS protocol through a Cloudflare Worker. The machines did not want to cooperate. They lost.
- 📅 Fixed a bug where **one missed punch turned every following day into a half day**. HR thanked me. Payroll thanked me more.
- 📬 Built a **Gmail client inside the ERP** because apparently opening a new tab is hard.
- 🤖 Built [**Strategy-AI**](https://strategy-ai-lac.vercel.app), an AI that reviews resumes. Yes, it reviewed mine. No, I won't share the score.
